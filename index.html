Media Experiments

Small browser experiments with webcams, hand tracking and live video filters. Everything runs on your device; no video leaves your browser.

Roblox Hand Portal

Frame a window with both hands in front of the webcam and see the video inside it through a Roblox-style filter, including one that turns you into a classic Roblox character. Pinch all five fingertips together to switch filters.

Filters in the Roblox Hand Portal

  1. Roblox Avatar Swaps your face for the classic yellow Roblox head with the default smile, on the old blue torso, yellow arms and green legs body.
  2. Studs Turns the video into bricks with round bumps on top, like the studs on Roblox’s classic building parts.
  3. Noob Paints everything in the three colours of the noob, Roblox’s classic starter look: yellow head, blue torso and green legs.
  4. Toon Flat colours and bold outlines, similar to the cartoony look of many Roblox games.
  5. Voxel Turns the image into big shaded cubes, like the blocky building parts and terrain in Roblox.
  6. Neon Obby Glowing coloured outlines, like the Neon material often used in Roblox obbies (obstacle courses).
  7. Lava Turns everything into moving lava, like the red “don’t touch” kill bricks in obbies and the popular game The Floor Is Lava.
  8. Oof Flashes red and shakes, like getting hurt in Roblox, named after the famous “oof” sound your character made when it died.
  9. BrickColor Snaps every colour to Roblox’s classic built-in part colours (called BrickColors) in chunky low-resolution blocks.
  10. Baseplate Turns the scene grey with grid lines, like the empty grey starting floor in Roblox Studio.
Needs a webcam and a recent desktop or mobile browser. Hand and face tracking by MediaPipe.
